Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: 15 September 2026

The CS2 POV Archive Meta app does not collect personal data from Facebook or Instagram viewers. It is used only to publish our own Page and Instagram content.

To request deletion of any personal information we hold about you (typically only if you emailed us, or if you authorised the app as an operator), email cs2povarchive@gmail.com with the subject “Data deletion request”. We will delete that information within 30 days and confirm by reply.

If you authorised this app with Facebook, you can also remove it under Facebook Settings → Apps and websites. We will then revoke any stored access tokens for that user.

Page posts and Reels we published as CS2 POV Archive are our own media and are not deleted on a viewer request. You can hide or unfollow that content in Facebook or Instagram.

See the Privacy Policy for full details.
