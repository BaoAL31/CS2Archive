Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 September 2026

This policy applies to the CS2 POV Archive Meta app (cs2povarchive) used to publish Counter-Strike 2 POV and highlight videos to our own Facebook Page and Instagram professional account.

Who we are

CS2 POV Archive publishes first-person demo footage of professional CS2 matches. Contact: cs2povarchive@gmail.com.

What data we collect

This app is operated only by us, on our own Page and Instagram account. It does not offer a login for the public and does not collect personal data from Facebook or Instagram viewers.

We process:

  • Page and Instagram IDs, access tokens, and publish metadata needed to list, schedule, and upload our own posts and Reels through Meta’s Graph API.
  • Video files, titles, and captions that we create.
  • Email sent to us if you write to the address above.

How we use it

Tokens and IDs are used only to authenticate Graph API calls that publish, schedule, list, or delete content on accounts we own. We do not sell data, run ads against viewer data, or share tokens with third parties except Meta (as required to operate the API).

How to request deletion

Email cs2povarchive@gmail.com with the subject “Data deletion request”. We will delete any personal information we hold about you (typically only if you emailed us) within 30 days, and confirm by reply. Content we published as CS2 POV Archive (Page posts and Reels) is our own media and is not deleted on a viewer request; you can hide or unfollow it in Facebook/Instagram.

If you are a Meta user who authorised this app (currently only our operators), we will also revoke stored access tokens for that user.

Terms of Service

The app exists solely to help CS2 POV Archive publish videos to its own Facebook Page and Instagram account. There is no public product, no user accounts, and no warranty. Meta’s Platform Terms also apply to any use of Facebook or Instagram APIs.

Do not use this app to post to Pages or Instagram accounts you do not own.
